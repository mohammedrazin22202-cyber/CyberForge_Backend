I’d explain V-Connect as a 'Digital Health Monitor' for the community. 

It takes complex data—like village complaints, costs, and wellness—and turns it into a simple, color-coded dashboard that tells them exactly where help is needed most. 

I avoid the tech jargon and focus on the business impact: we are using data to make better, faster decisions that improve lives. 

I bridge the gap between 'how it works' and 'why it matters,' which is the core of effective leadership communication.
