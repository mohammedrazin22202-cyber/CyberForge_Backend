The biggest challenge would be the shift from Python’s dynamic typing to Go’s strict static typing and its explicit concurrency model (goroutines). 

While Go offers much better runtime performance, it requires more upfront architectural planning. 

I view this as a trade-off between 'development speed' and 'execution efficiency'. 

For high-scale services, the performance gains of Go often justify the increased complexity, but it requires a disciplined mindset during the migration phase.
