LogiSense 360 is a predictive logistics analytics platform I developed during my experience at Al Haseena Exports Pvt. Ltd.

The platform automates shipment monitoring, forecasts delivery delays, and identifies spoilage risks to improve operational decision-making.

It integrates machine learning classification and regression models built using Scikit-learn with operational datasets, storing data in SQLite and MongoDB, and serving predictions through a Flask application with live KPI dashboards.
