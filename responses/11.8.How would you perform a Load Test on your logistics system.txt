I perform load tests using stress-testing tools to simulate thousands of concurrent users. 

I measure throughput and latency to find the system's breaking point. 

This identifies bottlenecks, such as slow DB queries or memory leaks, before they hit production. 

It’s about 'battle-hardening' the code. 

By pushing the system to its limit in a controlled environment, I ensure it can handle real-world logistics surges without performance degradation.
