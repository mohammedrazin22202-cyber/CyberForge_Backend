At a 10TB scale, vertical scaling is obsolete. 

I would implement Horizontal Partitioning (Sharding). For VConnect, I’d shard by Village ID. 

This keeps related records on the same node, minimizing cross-shard joins and ensuring query performance stays constant as the number of villages grows. 

I look at this as a risk management strategy: by distributing the load, we eliminate 'hotspots' and ensure a localized surge doesn't bring down the entire global registry.
