I view latency as a 'tax' on user experience. 

I implement Cache-Aside patterns using Redis to store frequently accessed data, reducing SQL hits by up to 80%. 

On the frontend, I leverage Browser Caching and ETags. 

My CA background makes me look at this from an 'efficiency-first' perspective: by reducing round-trip times, we aren't just improving speed; we are lowering compute costs and operational overhead at scale, which is essential for any high-volume application.
