Data cleaning is the process of identifying and fixing problems in raw data so it's reliable enough to analyze.

In practice: handling missing values (impute or drop — knowing when to do which), standardizing formats, removing duplicates, detecting outliers, and validating data against known constraints.

In VConnect, I built a multi-stage cleaning pipeline for 650K+ records. Each stage logged what it changed and why — at that scale you need an audit trail. You can't clean data silently and hope the outputs are right.

The principle: good cleaning is invisible. Bad cleaning destroys every analysis downstream.
