I use Recursive Feature Elimination and Lasso Regularization to strip away noise. 

The goal is to find the minimum set of features that provide the maximum predictive signal. 

I also lean on domain knowledge—my CA background helps me identify which financial indicators are logically relevant before I let the algorithm rank them. 

This results in a leaner, more interpretable model that is faster to train and less prone to overfitting on irrelevant data.
