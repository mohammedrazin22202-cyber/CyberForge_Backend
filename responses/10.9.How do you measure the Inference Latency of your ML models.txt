I measure inference latency as the total time from input receipt to prediction delivery. 

I use profiling tools to benchmark this in milliseconds. 

At a FAANG scale, even a 100ms delay can hurt UX. 

I optimize this by pruning models or using quantization to reduce the computational footprint. 

I view 'thinking time' as a resource cost—the more efficient the inference, the better the user experience and the lower the operational cost of the AI.
