I believe in Evidence-based Decision Making. 

In a recent project, a teammate and I disagreed on using SQL vs. NoSQL. Instead of arguing, I built a quick Benchmarking Suite for both. We measured query latency for complex joins versus document retrievals using 650k+ records. 

The data proved SQL was 30% faster for our specific reporting needs. 

The team aligned immediately because I replaced a subjective argument with objective data, which is the fastest way to achieve consensus in engineering.
