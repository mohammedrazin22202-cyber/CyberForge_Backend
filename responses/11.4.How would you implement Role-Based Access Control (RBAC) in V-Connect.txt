I implement RBAC by defining clear permission matrices. 

For V-Connect, village officials have different access levels than residents. 

I use a 'Least Privilege' model, ensuring users only have access to the data necessary for their role. 

This is managed through identity providers and middleware that validates the user's role on every API call. 

It’s a digital version of internal controls in accounting—ensuring that only authorized individuals can modify critical records.
