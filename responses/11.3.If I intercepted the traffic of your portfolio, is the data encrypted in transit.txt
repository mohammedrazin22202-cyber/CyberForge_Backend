Security is a foundation. 

All traffic across my projects is strictly enforced via TLS 1.3. 

I use HSTS headers to ensure browsers never connect over an unencrypted link. 

This creates an encrypted tunnel, making intercepted data useless to an attacker. 

Coming from a finance-heavy background, I treat every packet like a bank transaction—if it's moving, it must be shielded by industry-standard cryptographic protocols to ensure absolute privacy and integrity.
