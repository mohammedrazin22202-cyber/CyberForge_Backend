Phishing is a social engineering attack where someone is deceived into revealing sensitive information — passwords, credentials, financial data — by impersonating a trusted entity.

The attack surface is human psychology, not technical systems. A convincing fake email, a spoofed login page, a fraudulent SMS — the goal is to make the target act before they think critically.

It's one of the most common and effective attack vectors in cybersecurity. Not because it's technically sophisticated, but because humans are the hardest system to patch.

Awareness is the primary defense: know what phishing looks like, slow down, verify before you trust.
