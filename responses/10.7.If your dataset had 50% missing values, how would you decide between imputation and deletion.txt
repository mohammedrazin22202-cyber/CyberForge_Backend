With 50% missing data, I look for patterns. 

If a feature is missing at random, I use KNN Imputation to preserve the record's context. 

However, if the missingness is non-random, it often carries its own signal. 

I avoid mass deletion as it wastes valuable data. 

I treat missing values like 'missing ledger entries' in accounting—you don't just delete the transaction; you investigate the source and use the most logical estimation to maintain the integrity of the whole.
