I chose XGBoost over a simple Linear Regression because financial and demographic data is rarely linear—there are complex, hidden interactions between variables that a simple regression line just misses. 

My background in Chartered Accountancy has taught me that financial risk isn't a straight line; it’s a web of dependencies. 

XGBoost handles those non-linear patterns through its gradient boosting framework and provides built-in L1/L2 regularization, which was critical in preventing the model from overfitting on the smaller datasets we gathered during the pilot phase. 

It allowed me to prioritize model interpretability through feature importance rankings while maintaining a much higher predictive accuracy than traditional statistical models.
