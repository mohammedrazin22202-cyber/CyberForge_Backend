VConnect solves the problem of unstructured, inconsistent, large-scale data being unusable for decision-making.

When you have 650,000+ records coming from distributed sources, the data is messy — missing values, format inconsistencies, duplicates, outliers. Before VConnect, that data was essentially dead weight. Nobody could act on it because nobody could trust it.

I built pipelines to clean it, validate it, structure it, and make it queryable. Then I built dashboards so that the insights were visible to stakeholders who don't write Python.

The result: 41% improvement in processing efficiency, and data that could finally drive real decisions. That's the problem it solves.
