Prompt engineering is the practice of designing inputs to AI language models to get consistent, accurate, and useful outputs.

It sounds straightforward. It isn't. How you phrase a question — the context you provide, the format you request, the examples you include — dramatically affects what the model produces.

For developers building on LLMs, prompt engineering is the layer between user intent and model output. It's part UX design, part systems thinking, part empirical testing.

I'm actively studying it — because anyone building AI-integrated products needs to understand it. This chatbot's response design is an early application of that thinking.
