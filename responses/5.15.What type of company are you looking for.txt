A company where data is taken seriously — not just collected and ignored.

Specifically: an organization that uses data to drive real decisions, where the analytics function has visibility and influence, and where there's room to grow technically.

Size: open. Startups give you ownership and speed. Larger companies give you scale and structure. Both teach different things. The quality of the problem and the team matter more than the headcount.

Non-negotiable: work with people who are good at what they do and take it seriously.
