I once encountered a Python data-cleaning script that was choking on 600k+ records. 

By profiling the code, I found the bottleneck was in repeated list lookups. 

I replaced those with set-based lookups and used vectorized operations in Pandas, reducing execution time from 20 minutes to under 30 seconds. 

It taught me that 'brute force' code fails at scale. 

Optimization isn't just about speed; it's about making the most of every CPU cycle available.
