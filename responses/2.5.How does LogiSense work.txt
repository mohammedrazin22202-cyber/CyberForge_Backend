LogiSense 360 operates in three cohesive layers:

1. DATA LAYER (SQLite & MongoDB)
Stores historical logistics data and logs shipment events, allowing flexible schema design for rapid, high-velocity ingestion.

2. PREDICTIVE ENGINE (Python, Flask, Scikit-learn)
Applies regression and classification models to forecast delivery delays and detect spoilage/operational risks in real time.

3. DASHBOARD & ALERTS
Generates intelligent alerts for early exception handling and hosts custom KPI reporting interfaces for admins, drivers, and customers.
