I always analyze code through the lens of Computational Scalability. 

For village search, I moved from a O(n) linear search to a Hash-Map based lookup, achieving O(1) time complexity for direct ID retrievals. 

For partial matches, I utilize Indexed Search, keeping us at O(log n). 

I understand that at Google scale, O(n) isn't just 'slow'—it's an expensive drain on resources. 

By optimizing the growth rate, I ensure response times remain sub-millisecond, regardless of dataset size.
