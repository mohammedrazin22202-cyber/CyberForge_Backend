The honest answer: AI will replace the work, not the workers who adapt.

AI will automate the routine parts of software development. Boilerplate, basic scripts, simple CRUD APIs. If that's the full scope of your value, yes, that's at risk.

But developers who understand systems deeply — who can architect solutions, evaluate whether AI output is actually correct, and use AI as a force multiplier — those people become more valuable, not less.

My view: the floor for what "developer" means is rising fast. The ceiling is higher than it's ever been. Adapt, or be replaced by someone who does.
