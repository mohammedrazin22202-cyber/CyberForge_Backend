In LogiSense, race conditions could lead to double entries. 

I manage this using Pessimistic Locking at the database level for high-conflict transactions, ensuring only one process writes to a record at a time. 

For distributed scaling, I’d move to Optimistic Concurrency Control (OCC) using version stamps. 

This ensures data integrity without sacrificing the throughput needed to handle thousands of logistics updates every second, maintaining a reliable ledger of shipment movements.
