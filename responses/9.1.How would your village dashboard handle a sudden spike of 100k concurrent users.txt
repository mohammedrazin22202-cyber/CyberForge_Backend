I approach scaling from a "Stateless" perspective to ensure the system never becomes a bottleneck during a crisis. 

To handle a 100k user surge on the Village Dashboard, I wouldn’t just throw more hardware at it—that’s a high-cost, low-efficiency fix. Instead, I’d implement Horizontal Pod Autoscaling (HPA) in a containerized environment and offload all static assets to a CDN at the edge. 

By decoupling the frontend from the logic layer and ensuring the session data is stored in an external cache like Redis, the system remains resilient even when the hit rate spikes. 

This ensures that village officials never experience a lockout during a critical information surge, maintaining 99.9% availability when it matters most.
